> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Azure AD External Identities P2 is retired in Azure AD B2C tenants.** ID Protection capabilities are also no longer available in Azure AD B2C P1 tenants, including the Risky users and Risk detections reports. ID Protection as part of P2 remains available in workforce tenants. For details on billing and alternatives, see the [P2 retirement FAQ](../articles/active-directory-b2c/faq.yml#azure-ad-external-identities-p2-retirement).
