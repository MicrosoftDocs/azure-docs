> [!WARNING]
> The Dapr extension for Azure Functions, previously in public preview, is retired and unsupported. It's not included in main extension bundle versions 4.43.0 and later or preview extension bundle versions 4.51.0 and later.
>
> Migrate to Dapr client SDKs or Dapr HTTP APIs, and replace Dapr triggers with HTTP-triggered functions. See [Migrate from the Dapr extension](../articles/azure-functions/functions-bindings-dapr.md#migrate-from-the-dapr-extension).
