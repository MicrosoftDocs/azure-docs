# View Advisor recommendations in Azure Workload Center for SAP

Advisor recommendations in Azure Workload Center surface actionable, workload‑aware guidance directly in the context where you manage your critical workloads. By integrating Azure Advisor insights across reliability, security, and performance, Azure Workload Center helps you quickly identify risks, optimize configurations, and apply best practices without leaving your workload view.

1.  Navigate to Advisor recommendations.

    From the Azure Workload Center left navigation or from within the selected workload’s menu, open Advisor recommendations.

2.  Review recommendations.

    The Advisor recommendations view displays guidance along with details.

    :::image type="content" source="media/view-advisor-recommendation/advisor-recommendations-list-view.png" alt-text="Screenshot of Advisor recommendations page listing three recommendations with impact and completion progress." lightbox="media/view-advisor-recommendation/advisor-recommendations-list-view.png":::

3.  Select a recommendation to understand its impact and recommended action. Follow the provided links to remediate or optimize the workload configuration.
