# App Service guardrails initiative
This article describes the Policy guardrails in place to ensure Azure App Service is deployed securely.

## Initiative details

Azure Enclave assigns the App Service guardrails initiative (policy set definition ID `528d78c5-246c-4f26-ade6-d30798705411`) to workload resource groups.

The `Effect` values in the tables show the effects supported by the policy definitions. The effective behavior of a policy assignment can vary based on Azure Enclave policy configuration and policy parameters.
