# BeeKeeperAI, Inc.

BeeKeeperAI is a confidential computing software company. Its EscrowAI platform provides confidential and verifiable execution workflows for AI -- enforcing the actions and outputs of models and agents inside Azure confidential computing's hardware-isolated Trusted Execution Environment (TEEs), with an immutable, tamper-proof record of each computing cycle. 

EscrowAI enables organizations to execute proprietary models and agents on protected data without exposing either the underlying data or the intellectual property. Born out of the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) Center for Digital Health Innovation, EscrowAI has been in commercial production for 4+ years and is protected by over a dozen U.S. patents.

## Confidence. Control. Compliance. 

EscrowAI delivers value to everyone at the table:
Chief AI Officer - Confidence - Bring third-party models and agents in, or send your proprietary models out, without exposing IP, to support the AI lifecycle (from training to deployment). 
Chief Data Officer - Control - Put protected data to work without ever losing custody or sovereignty - or exposing it. 
Chief Information Officer - Compliance - Approve the policy-aligned run — EscrowAI attests before it computes, enforces during, and logs after: proof of compliance, not promises.

## Confidential & Verifiable AI Execution Capabilities 

EscrowAI's confidential execution workflow protects data, models, and agents during computation and provides an immutable record of what happened. The capabilities below are how EscrowAI delivers confidential execution across the AI lifecycle.

Enforced AI Operating Boundary & Immutable Record 
EscrowAI enforces the actions and outputs of models and agents inside your controlled boundary. Every computing cycle is captured in an immutable record you can audit.

Sovereign data protection 
Sensitive data stays under the data custodian's control throughout computation inside the TEE — encrypted throughout and inaccessible even to EscrowAI.

End-to-end IP protection 
The model remains under the model owner's authority throughout execution and is never exposed or extractable by the data custodian or EscrowAI.

Automated multi-party collaboration 
EscrowAI automates the permissions, approvals, policies, and controls that let data custodians and model developers collaborate securely from development through use — without either party surrendering control of its data, models, or agents.

Financial Services – Underwriting and fraud-detection models depend on sensitive customer and transaction data. EscrowAI lets institutions train and deploy models without exposing the underlying dataset or scoring logic.

Other Regulated Industries – EscrowAI is relevant for any organization where AI needs to compute on data too sensitive, proprietary, or regulated to expose.
