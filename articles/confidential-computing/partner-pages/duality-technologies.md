# Duality Technologies

## Overview

[Duality Technologies](https://dualitytech.com/) provides a secure data collaboration platform that organizations can use to run analytics and AI on sensitive data they can't move, pool, or share, powered by [Azure confidential computing](../index.yml).

Rather than relying on a single technique, Duality applies the protection each workload calls for: confidential computing and trusted execution environments, fully homomorphic encryption, federated learning, and differential privacy. A trusted execution environment secures the environment a workload runs in, while cryptographic protection travels with the data across boundaries a single environment doesn't cover. Participants compute a shared result while each dataset stays under its owner's controls - across departments and data domains inside one institution as well as between separate organizations.

Duality is available on the Azure Marketplace.

## Duality for healthcare and life sciences

Duality makes cross-institution clinical studies possible where privacy rules would otherwise block them. Each site keeps custody and control of its own records, and those records stay protected while they're analyzed, so research that couldn't previously obtain approval becomes approvable.

The platform's Zero Footprint Query capability runs SQL-style queries directly on encrypted patient records without decrypting them, and the architecture addresses regulatory requirements in a single deployment. Duality supports multi-hospital clinical trial recruitment - identifying eligible cohorts without any site exposing its records - as well as population health analytics across institutions and real-world evidence generation on siloed clinical, genomic, and RWE datasets.

## Duality for financial services and government

Competing financial institutions can run fraud detection and anti-money laundering analysis across institutions, surfacing patterns no single bank sees on its own, with each participant's transaction data encrypted and under its own control. Duality also supports KYC compliance workflows and privacy-preserving identity verification across branches, divisions, and external entities, and runs encrypted inference for structured data such as fraud scoring and credit risk models.

For government and defense organizations, Duality enables cross-agency and cross-department analytics without centralizing data, so each agency or department keeps custody of its own records while contributing to a shared result. The platform fits a zero-trust architecture by keeping data protected while it's in use, so no infrastructure, operator, or environment is implicitly trusted.
