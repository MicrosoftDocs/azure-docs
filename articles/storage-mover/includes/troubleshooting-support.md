## Troubleshooting and support

Troubleshooting your migration might involve a range of steps, from basic diagnostics to more advanced error handling. If you encounter problems, start troubleshooting by taking the following steps:
-	Migration job failed? Check the logs for error messages.
-	Permission problems? Verify that Access Management (IAM) roles have the correct access.
